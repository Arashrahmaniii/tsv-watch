Venues

Venues on the SEC's list of Tokenized Securities Venues. Being on the list means a venue filed a notice; the SEC states that inclusion does not confirm a venue meets the exemption. It is not an approval.

Method

How it is built

  • Every day TSV Watch downloads each venue's public notice, including versions linked only from the venue's scripts, and keeps each version by its SHA-256 hash.
  • Objections are read from each notice's own list of objecting issuers. Tickers come from the notice; where a notice gives only a name, the ticker is matched to the US symbol directory.
  • A run fails visibly if a venue's page lists a notice newer than anything archived.
  • Data files are listed in a manifest signed with Ed25519 (see verify/). A valid signature proves the files are unchanged since signing; check facts against the linked notices.

What this is not

  • Not a deadline calculator. The 30-day objection window runs from the day an issuer receives the venue's Issuer Notice, and receipt dates are not public.
  • Not an approval list. The SEC's venue list records that a notice was filed.
  • Not a statement about any token's rights or any company's position beyond what its objection notice shows.
  • Not legal or investment advice.

Beyond the list

The full register, for venues, issuers and data teams

  • Issuers: one alert feed per stock for new venue notices that name it, new tokens, corporate-action changes and halts, plus an objection draft filled from EDGAR for counsel.
  • Venues: the combined objection list before you send an Issuer Notice, a halt mirror, prior-month volume caps by LULD tier, and a public trade tape built from Uniswap v4 swaps.
  • Tokenizers, oracles and data providers: daily signed snapshots of supply and multipliers for every tokenized-stock contract, and split checks across tokenizers and chains.
